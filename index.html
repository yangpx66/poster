ALBA · Towards MRI-compatible bladder assistance

Simulating ALBA contraction and bladder interaction

Three animations explore the mechanics at different scales — from contraction of the Hoberman-inspired auxetic dome to deformation of the silicone phantom and its local interaction with an actuation button.
